# Trademark Registration Guide (IP India / Trade Marks Registry)
## Under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 | Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM)

To protect the brand identity, name, and visual mark of **MedScript** and **MedScript OPD** against unauthorized commercial use across India.

---

### 1. Trademark Details & Classification (Nice Classification)

#### Application 1: Word Mark
- **Proposed Word Mark**: `MEDSCRIPT` and `MEDSCRIPT OPD`
- **Class 9 (Core Software Product)**:
  > *Description of Goods/Services*: Downloadable and recorded computer software for medical clinic management, electronic medical records (EMR), outpatient prescription generation, drug interaction surveillance, and healthcare diagnostic record maintenance; software for clinical practice automation and digital health record authentication.
- **Class 42 (Software Development & Cloud Services)**:
  > *Description of Goods/Services*: Software as a Service (SaaS) featuring software for medical clinic operations, design and development of healthcare IT systems, clinical data management software maintenance, electronic health record data security consultancy, and technical support services for medical software.

### 3. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure on IP India Portal
1. **Portal**: Go to [https://ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in).
2. **Class 3 DSC**: Acquire a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for Dr. Nitin Sonare (used for GST, MCA, and IP India).
3. **User Account**: Register as an "Applicant / Natural Person" (or MSME if registered under Udyam).
4. **Select Form**: Select **Form TM-A** (Application for Registration of a Trademark).
5. **Fee Structure**:
   - **Individual / Startup / Small Enterprise (MSME)**: **₹4,500** per class.
   - **Others / Companies**: ₹9,000 per class.
   - *(Tip: Registering a free Udyam certificate saves ₹4,500 per class immediately).*
6. **Filing Status & Symbol**:
   - Upon filing and payment, you immediately receive an official **Application Number** and can start using the **™** symbol next to MedScript.
   - Once the examination is complete, published in the Trademark Journal, and registered, you receive the **®** registration certificate.
